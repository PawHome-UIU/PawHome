===============================================================================
PAWHOME - PROJECT PROPOSAL
Presentation speech for slides 1 to 8
CSE 3522 - Database Management Systems Laboratory
===============================================================================

  About 470 words. Roughly 3 minutes at a calm speaking pace.
  Short sentences, simple words. Nothing hard to say out loud.
  Text in (brackets) is timing. Do not read it.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 1 - TITLE                                                        (10 sec)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Good morning.

We are presenting PawHome.

It is a pet adoption and boarding management system.

This is our project proposal.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 2 - ELEVATOR PITCH                                               (35 sec)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Here is the problem in one line.

Animal shelters still run on paper. And adopters still choose a pet by
looking at a photo. So many pets come back.

PawHome puts both sides on one website.

A shelter manages its animals, its boarding rooms and its health records
in one place.

An adopter answers a short quiz about their daily life. The system then
matches them to the right pet.

They can apply, book boarding, and get vaccination reminders - all on the
same site.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 3 - THE TEAM                                                     (32 sec)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We are a team of two, and we took one journey each.

I take "finding a home" - the catalogue, the adopter and staff screens,
accounts and login, applications and adoptions. That is eleven tables.

My teammate takes "looking after a pet" - the quiz, boarding, daily care,
vaccinations and reports. That is sixteen tables.

So we did not split it as one person on the database and one on the
features. Each of us builds the screens, the API and the tables for our
own part.

The course asks for four to five members. We are two, so we would like
your approval on this.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 4 - TYPE OF PROJECT                                              (18 sec)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PawHome is a web application.

It runs in any browser, so there is nothing to install.

It has three types of users.

And behind it there is one MySQL database - twenty-seven tables and
fifty-three relationships.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 5 - OBJECTIVES                                                   (28 sec)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We have four objectives.

First, give a shelter one place to manage everything.

Second, match adopters by lifestyle, not by looks.

Third, keep health records and send vaccination reminders automatically.

And fourth - put the rules inside the database itself. So even if our code
makes a mistake, the database still refuses bad data.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 6 - PROBLEM STATEMENT                                            (27 sec)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

These are the four problems we found.

Shelter records live on paper, so nothing can be searched.

Adopters choose by photo, then the pet does not fit their home, and it is
returned.

Boarding is booked by phone, so there is no record of the daily care.

And vaccination dates are written on a card, so they get missed.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 7 - DELIVERABLES                                                 (20 sec)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We will deliver six things.

A public catalogue anyone can browse.

The matching quiz.

Three portals - one for adopters, one for shelter staff, one for admin.

And the MySQL database, with its migrations and sample data.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 8 - METHODOLOGY                                                  (28 sec)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We are following Agile, in three increments.

First we built every screen, so we could agree on the design before
locking the data model.

Second, the database and the login system.

Third, the features on top.

We chose Agile because the schema changed as soon as we saw real screens.
It was cheaper to find that out in week two than in week ten.


===============================================================================
DELIVERY NOTES
===============================================================================

  Slide 2 - Stand still and do not click. This is the part they remember.

  Slide 3 - Two things here. First, make the split clear: it is one
            journey each, not one person on the database and one on the
            features. Both of us own tables. Second, say the team-size
            line yourself. Do not wait to be asked. Raising it first
            sounds like planning. Being asked about it sounds like an
            oversight.

  Slide 4 - Do not read the numbers twice. Say them once, then move on.

  Slide 8 - The last two lines are the reason we chose Agile. Slow down
            there; it is the sentence a teacher is most likely to follow
            up on.

  Everywhere - Pause for one second between slides. It sounds confident,
               and it gives you time to breathe.
