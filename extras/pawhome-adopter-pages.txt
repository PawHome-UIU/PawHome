PAWHOME — ADOPTER PORTAL
What each page does
================================================================================

The adopter portal lives under /app. The sidebar has seven entries; three more
pages are reached from inside them, and the header carries two more.

Everything below reads and writes the real database. No page uses sample data.


================================================================================
1. DASHBOARD                                                             /app
================================================================================
The landing page. Four counts and the top of three lists.

  - Four tiles, each a link to its own page:
      Active Applications ... only ones still waiting for an answer
      Favourites Saved
      Recommended Matches .... only pets that share at least one trait with you
      Active Boardings ....... accepted or currently staying
  - Top three recommended pets, with working hearts
  - Your three most recent applications, each with a progress rail
      (Submitted -> Under review -> Decision)
  - Your next boarding stay, with a line saying how many days until drop-off,
    or that the pet is staying now
  - Greets you by your real first name

If you have not taken the personality quiz, the recommendations block is
replaced by a prompt to take it, rather than showing everything at 0%.


================================================================================
2. SHELTER PETS                                                     /app/pets
================================================================================
The catalogue of animals available to adopt.

  - Search by name or breed
  - Filter by species, size and gender
  - Sort by best match or by name
  - A heart on every card to save or unsave
  - Each card shows the pet's photo, breed, age and match percentage

Only AVAILABLE pets appear. Pets people own from home never appear here.


================================================================================
3. PET PROFILE                                                  /app/pets/:id
================================================================================
One animal in full.

  - Photo gallery with thumbnails
  - Breed, age, sex, size, weight, adoption fee, description
  - Personality traits, and your match score with the traits you share
  - Health card: vaccinated / neutered / microchipped, plus the vaccination
    list with dates and anything overdue
  - Shelter name and city
  - Save to favourites
  - Apply to adopt

The vet's name, batch numbers and the certificate document are not shown here.
Those stay with the pet's owner and the shelter holding it.


================================================================================
4. ADOPTION APPLICATION                                   /app/pets/:id/apply
================================================================================
The form you send to a shelter.

  - Take the eligibility quiz first; the form will not submit without it
  - Your details: name, email, phone, address, city
  - Your home: home type, time at home, pet experience, other pets or children
  - The score comes from your quiz attempt on the server, never from the form

Your answers are stored as a copy on the application, so what the shelter reads
later is what you said when you sent it, even if you move house afterwards.


================================================================================
5. RECOMMENDATIONS                                       /app/recommendations
================================================================================
Pets ranked against your personality quiz answers.

  - Take or retake the personality quiz
  - Your traits shown as chips
  - Top two matches as large cards, each listing which of your traits the pet
    has and which it does not
  - The rest as a grid, best match first
  - Hearts work here too

Without a quiz attempt the whole page becomes a prompt to take it.


================================================================================
6. MY APPLICATIONS                                          /app/applications
================================================================================
Everything you have applied for and where each one got to.

  - Tabs: All / Pending / Approved / Declined / Withdrawn
  - Each row: pet, shelter, date applied, status
  - Detail panel with your eligibility score
  - Withdraw an application that has not been decided yet
  - If declined, the shelter's written reason is shown in full
  - If approved, the adoption date and the fee paid


================================================================================
7. MY PETS                                                      /app/my-pets
================================================================================
Every animal you own — adopted through PawHome, or one you already had.

  - Add a pet from home: name, species, breed, age, sex, size, weight,
    neutered, microchipped, description
  - Edit or remove a pet you added yourself
  - Photos: upload several, choose the cover, delete one
  - Health: vaccination records, and the certificate as a download

Pets adopted through PawHome are read-only here. Their record belongs to the
shelter that rehomed them, so you can view their health and photos but not
change them. A pet cannot be removed once it has any boarding history.


================================================================================
8. BOARDING                                                     /app/boarding
================================================================================
Booking a stay while you travel.

  - Your pets, each with a Request Boarding button
  - Step 1: the boarding quiz
  - Step 2: the booking form
      shelter, drop-off and collection dates, emergency contact,
      whether you authorise emergency vet care,
      care instructions (dietary, medication, comfort and play, exercise),
      an optional vaccination certificate for a pet from home
  - A running cost estimate: nights x the shelter's nightly rate
  - History of every stay you have requested, with its status


================================================================================
9. BOARDING DETAIL                                          /app/boarding/:id
================================================================================
One stay, while it happens.

  - Progress rail: Requested -> Accepted -> Active -> Completed
  - Nights, assigned suite, estimated cost
  - Your care instructions, grouped by category
  - Live updates from the shelter — feeding, playtime, medication, health —
    with photos, newest first
  - Shelter address and phone
  - Cancel a stay that has not started

If the shelter declined, their written reason is shown here instead of the rail.


================================================================================
10. FAVOURITES                                                 /app/favorites
================================================================================
Pets you saved.

  - Sort by recently saved, name, or match score
  - Remove from favourites
  - Adopted and unavailable pets stay in the list rather than vanishing, so you
    find out what happened to them


================================================================================
11. SETTINGS                                                    /app/settings
================================================================================
Your account. Three tabs.

  Profile        name, phone, profile picture
                 (email is read-only — changing it needs re-verification)
  Security       change your password
                 doing so signs out every other device immediately
  Notifications  application updates, boarding updates, vaccination reminders,
                 and whether any of it also reaches your inbox
                 saved the moment you flip a switch


================================================================================
IN THE HEADER — ON EVERY PAGE
================================================================================
  Bell     Unread count, and a dropdown of recent notifications. New ones
           arrive live without refreshing the page. Click one to jump to what
           it is about. Mark one or all as read.

           You are notified when an application is approved or declined, when
           a boarding request is accepted or refused, and every time the
           shelter posts an update about a pet staying with them.

  Gear     Settings.


================================================================================
NOT BUILT FOR ADOPTERS
================================================================================
  - Messaging a shelter. There is no messages table in the database, so rather
    than show a button that does nothing, the shelter's phone number is given
    on the boarding detail page.
  - Pagination. Every list returns everything at once, which is fine at the
    sizes this project handles.
